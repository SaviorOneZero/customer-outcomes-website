01 / The gap

Companies know what they sold. Can they prove the customer achieved what they bought?

CRM, product, support, and delivery systems are good at recording what happened. They tell you what was sold, what was delivered, how the product is being used, and where problems have surfaced.

Activity is not evidence of value. A customer can be fully deployed and highly active without achieving the business result that justified the purchase.

The problem is usually discovered too late. By then the renewal is already in motion, the team is trying to reconstruct the value story, and expansion is harder to justify.

02 / The Promise Arc

How PromiseArc works

Start with why the customer bought. Define what success actually means, keep the path and evidence visible, and step in when things drift off course.

  1. 01

    Promise

    Identify the business result that justified the investment, not just the product or SKU.

  2. 02

    Outcome

    Turn that promise into an outcome the team can actually track.

  3. 03

    Value Path

    Map what has to happen before the outcome can be achieved, including the work, adoption, dependencies, and owners.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Capture the evidence that shows whether the outcome is actually happening.

  5. 05

    Cadence

    Review progress and evidence throughout the customer relationship, not just at renewal.

  6. 06

    Decide & Act

    When something is off track, make the next decision and owner clear.

03 / Managing customer value

Manage the relationship around outcomes, not activity alone.

THE USUAL VIEW
  • What did we sell?
  • Did we implement it?
  • Are they using it?
  • Is the customer happy?
  • What is their health score?
→
THE PROMISEARC VIEW
  • What outcome did the customer purchase?
  • What must happen to achieve it?
  • What evidence proves progress?
  • Where is value at risk?
  • What should we do next?
  • Do we have the evidence to support renewal or expansion?

Evidence over scores

If a customer is healthy or at risk, you should be able to explain why and point to the evidence.
