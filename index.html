01 / The gap

Companies know what they sold. Can they prove the customer achieved what they bought?

CRM, product, support, and delivery systems can show what was contracted, implemented, used, discussed, and escalated.

Activity is not evidence of value. A customer can be fully deployed and highly active without achieving the business result that justified the purchase.

If that gap stays hidden, teams discover it late. Renewal conversations become reactive, executives lack a clear account of value, and expansion is harder to support.

Renewal surprisesWeak executive conversationsUnclear accountabilityMissed expansion

02 / The Promise Arc

How PromiseArc works

PromiseArc starts with why the customer bought and defines what success should look like. It tracks the path to that outcome, records the evidence, and helps teams act when progress slips.

  1. 01

    Promise

    Identify the business result that justified the investment, not just the product or SKU.

  2. 02

    Outcome

    Define clear, measurable outcomes for the customer.

  3. 03

    Value Path

    Set out the milestones, changes, adoption, dependencies, and stakeholders needed to reach each outcome.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Record clear evidence of progress and value achieved.

  5. 05

    Cadence

    Review progress and evidence throughout the customer relationship, not just at renewal.

  6. 06

    Decide & Act

    See what is at risk, where there is opportunity, and who needs to act next.

03 / Managing customer value

Manage customers around outcomes, not activity alone.

THE USUAL VIEW
  • What did we sell?
  • Did we implement it?
  • Are they using it?
  • Is the customer happy?
  • What is their health score?
→
THE PROMISEARC VIEW
  • What outcome did the customer purchase?
  • What must happen to achieve it?
  • What evidence proves progress?
  • Where is value at risk?
  • What should we do next?
  • Can we defend renewal—or expansion?

Evidence over scores

You should be able to explain why a customer is healthy, why an outcome is at risk, and what evidence supports that conclusion.

Renewal should not be the first moment you discover whether a customer received value.
