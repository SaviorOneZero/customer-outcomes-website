01 / The gap

Companies know what they sold. Can they prove the customer achieved what they bought?

CRM, product, support, and delivery systems are good at recording what happened. They tell you what was sold, what was delivered, how the product is being used, and where problems have surfaced.

Activity is not evidence of value. A customer can be fully deployed and highly active without achieving the business result that justified the purchase.

The problem is usually discovered too late. By then the renewal is already in motion, the team is trying to reconstruct the value story, and expansion is harder to justify.

Renewal surprisesWeak executive conversationsUnclear accountabilityMissed expansion

02 / The systems gap

Your systems hold the facts. They do not automatically prove the Outcome.

CRM holds the commercial record. Services and delivery systems track work completed. Product data shows usage and adoption. Support records friction. Customer Success platforms track engagement, workflow, and health.

Each matters. None, on its own, proves that the customer achieved the business Outcome behind the purchase.

SIGNALS FROM EXISTING SYSTEMS
  • What was sold and when it renews
  • What was implemented or delivered
  • Who is using the product and how
  • Where support issues have surfaced
  • What the account team is doing

03 / The Promise Arc

How PromiseArc works

Start with why the customer bought. Define what success means, track the evidence, and act when progress slips.

Delivered is not the same as adopted. Adopted is not the same as Value realized. The model keeps those conditions separate so teams can see where progress has stopped.

  1. 01

    Promise

    Identify the business result that justified the investment, not just the product or SKU.

  2. 02

    Outcome

    Turn the original promise into an observable, measurable result.

  3. 03

    Value Path

    Map the work, adoption, dependencies, stakeholders, and other conditions needed to reach the Outcome.

  4. 04

    Evidence

    Record the proof that shows progress and realized Value. Keep missing evidence visible too.

  5. 05

    Cadence

    Review progress and evidence throughout the customer relationship, not just at renewal.

  6. 06

    Decide & Act

    When something is off track, make the next decision, action, and owner clear.

04 / The product

The customer-outcome decision layer.

PromiseArc keeps the commercial picture, Outcomes, Value Paths, Evidence, Risks, and accountable Actions together.

It is designed to work alongside CRM, Customer Success, Support, delivery, and product systems, not replace them. Those systems remain the source of the facts. PromiseArc connects those facts to the Outcome and the decisions that follow.

05 / How organizations start

Start with the customer-outcome operating model.

An Outcome Assessment shows where the thread from Promise to Outcome breaks today: at handoff, in ownership, in the definition of success, or in the Evidence used to judge progress.

Technology follows the operating model. We make the model clear first, test it with real accounts, and then configure PromiseArc around it.

01

Understand the current process

Review how teams handle the sale, handoff, delivery, value review, renewal, and expansion today.

02

Find the gaps

Identify where Outcomes, ownership, Evidence, or decisions become unclear across the customer lifecycle.

03

Define the framework

Agree how the organization will define Outcomes, Value Paths, Evidence, Risk, Actions, and review cadence.

04

Test representative accounts

Apply the framework to a small set of real customer relationships and adjust what does not hold up.

05

Configure PromiseArc

Set up the approved operating model in the platform, including ownership, views, and workflow.

06

Establish the cadence

Put the model into regular account, portfolio, executive, and renewal conversations.

06 / Best fit
